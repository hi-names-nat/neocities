# Entry \#12 - Under Burning Skies
## 7/20/2026

It is happening again.

The sky tinged orange with flame, The sun snuffed out with smoke. A pleasant(?) campfireishness in the air. For at least the second year in a row, the world is burning.

Much has changed in my world in the year between flames, some of which has been documented here, much of it which has not. When do I have the time to sit down and process
what I've been thinking, what I've been feeling, what I've been doing? I haven't had that luxury in months. My time I have not been fighting to keep everything afloat is
spent bandaging my wounds so I can do it all again the next day. Now, however, is a somewhat special time. Thing for the moment have calmed. The tide rough but no longer
looking to capsize the little ship that is my life. Now is a time for reflection, so as painful as it may be, I shall try.

The last year this happened, I was much more able to reflect. I was indeed doing a lot. Running a community, meeting creatures, trying to put something together for those I cared about,
and meeting new creatures who I felt the same way towards. I was doing things I cared about, putting together something for others. Trying hard to do things I was proud
of, things that I believed in. 

this year, as the smoke returns, I find myself hanging on for dear life to try to keeep everything functioning. The community I tried to build has fallen into disrepair
due to my absence. Creatures I trusted stabbed me in the back only to put themselves ahead, I was left to make an impossible situation work. To land from freefall
when my feet hadn't been on the ground a second. To lose home, transport, things cherished, and most of all to lose a partner, have them drop me like a sack of
rocks, after watching the same thing happen, knowing they know how much pain they are infliciting. It is without measure, it is an insult. It is painful.

But. Somehow I am alive. Between difficult decision and difficult decision to keep myself and those who I was stuck with somewhat safe, including having to make the
decision I could not afford to keep one of them around, I am alive. My relationships are worse for it. I do not know anything of what is happening anymore. I have
strained each and every relationship I have, and I know not if the damage has worn its way to the foundation, but. I am here, I have managed. I am alive. I ride a motorcycle now. I get
to have time with my wife again. I have a place to rest my head, and a place to call home. I have things to look forward to, partners who want to see me. I'm looking
to put myself on a better foothold in order to hopefully find my own place, soon.

Things are still difficult. Things will be difficult for a very long time from now on, I do not believe there to be a world where things can be as they once were. I will
always have more work to do. I struggle with the place where I live. I do not feel respected. I need to find a better job in order to make moves. I've been struggling to set myself up financially
for months, and every time it feels like I'm okay now, something else happens. I have to keep making decisions that I don't want to make, that I shouldn't have to make,
that others do not have to make. It seems I am always at the point of the sword, fighting for my life where for others it is simply tuesday. But I must survive, I must.
If not for me, then for them. Those who I care about so incredibly. Those who are the world to me. One day perhaps they will decide I am not worth the trouble, and that
day perhaps my world and priorities change, but as long as dragons rely on me, I will be there to lend wing and fur, even if it is broken and bloody.

It's my role, under burning skies.
